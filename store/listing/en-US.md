## Full description (max 4000)
Listo is a fast, free shopping list that keeps itself tidy. Type what you need and
Listo files each item under the right aisle — produce, dairy, bakery, meat & fish,
drinks, pantry and more — so the list is already sorted by the time you reach the shop.

WHY LISTO
• Type it, don't organise it. Items are sorted into categories automatically.
• Tick as you shop. A progress bar shows how far along you are, and ticked items
  drop to the bottom so what's left stays on top.
• Know what it costs. Add a price to any item and set a budget for the list —
  Listo shows what's left, or how far over you've gone.
• Plan ahead. Give a list a date and an optional reminder, then see the whole
  month on the built-in calendar.
• Keep a record. Finished lists move to History instead of vanishing.
• Share in a tap. Send any list to someone as plain text.

MADE TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY
• No account, no sign-up, no cloud. Your lists stay on your phone.
• Works fully offline.
• Light, dark or follow-the-system theme, plus fifteen accent colours tuned for easy reading.
• Available in English, Türkçe and Svenska.

FREE
Listo is free and supported by a single small banner ad. There is nothing to buy,
no subscription and no paywalled feature.

PERMISSIONS
• Notifications — only to deliver the reminders you set yourself.
• Internet — to load the banner ad.
• Run at startup — to restore your reminders after the phone reboots.

Listo is open source. Feedback and issues are welcome on GitHub.
